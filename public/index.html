CHE-110 Project Academic Methodology

Environmental Chemistry & Life-Cycle Analysis (LCA) Engineering Foundation

Fundamental Emission Formula

E_total = Σ (Activity_i × Emission_Factor_i) × (1 - Mitigation_Factor)

Where activity metrics (kWh, km, diet days) are converted to global warming potential in kg CO₂ equivalent.

Grid Carbon Intensity

Uses Indian CEA / IPCC emission intensity of 0.72 kg CO₂e / kWh for thermal dominated grid mix, with 85% clean mitigation for solar induction.
